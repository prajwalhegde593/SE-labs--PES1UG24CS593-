## Lab 2 summary

Scoped to a single epic as instructed. **Epic 1 — WIP Draft Upload & Watermark Protection** carries
FR-001, the watermarking requirement named in the problem statement, together with the WIP upload
half of FR-003 and the draft review portion of FR-004. It holds six user stories totalling 34 story
points, estimated on the Fibonacci scale.

Two sprints were simulated in Jira (28 Aug – 11 Sep, two weeks each): Sprint 1 committed and completed
19 points, Sprint 2 committed and completed 15. All 34 points were delivered and the epic closed with
an empty backlog.
